Data sources

Four sources: (1) Met Éireann warnings, (2) the council Sheet, (3) Open-Meteo flood API: Copernicus GloFAS modelled river discharge, forecast and history from the same model, (4) ten years of gauge water-level history from waterlevel.ie (CSV files, summarised once and stored in this page). Flow (m³/s) and gauge level (mAOD) are different units and are never mixed: flow decides the nightly level; gauge history answers the Area Manager's question and gives a level to watch.
